Grant & Partnership Strategy

SRG is positioned for government grants by centering justice-involved returning citizens, education pathways, and compliance-first programming with measurable outcomes.

Target Grant Categories

  • Second Chance Act & reentry services for returning citizens
  • Legal access, civil rights education, and due process literacy
  • Education and workforce pipelines for justice-impacted people

Credibility Safeguards

  • Institutional language and compliance documentation
  • Measurable outputs: certifications, filings assisted, grievances resolved
  • Shared governance and internal controls

College & University Anchors

HBCU and paralegal partnerships add accreditation credibility, unlock education funding, and support fiscal sponsor or joint-program models.

Immigration Legal Literacy

Provide know-your-rights education, identify immigration holds early, and route members to accredited legal partners without providing individualized legal advice.

Public Trust & Perception

Keep messaging education-first, emphasize lawful process, and document partnerships to minimize institutional risk and maximize grant confidence.

Program Protection

Separate leadership narrative from compliance narrative to strengthen neutrality and unlock multi-agency collaboration.

Important Notice

SRG provides legal literacy and referral support. We do not provide legal representation or individualized legal advice.
